# ERP.pdf business map

Source: the user-supplied **服装外贸公司 ERP 核心模块 & 内部数据清单（外贸服装行业专用，区别内销 ERP）**, two pages, canonical source `docs/references/ERP.pdf` in the platform repository. This reference preserves its thirteen-module business requirements for the distributed plugin; it does not require access to a local repository to operate the Skill. Tool availability and actual returned facts determine executable coverage, not this business checklist. Coverage below reflects the current plugin/server contracts, not business acceptance of complete modules.

| PDF module | Source facts to preserve | Current execution / boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. 基础档案 / master data | Customers: country, contacts, email/phone, T/T/L/C/D/P, FOB/CIF/EXW, currency, credit, customs buyer identity, shipping marks, code, grade, tax-refund entity. Suppliers: fabric, trim, process, garment factory, forwarder and test institution, terms, banking, contacts, quotes, quality. Styles: style/customer numbers, season, category, image, English name, sizes/ratios, colors/PANTONE/swatches. Materials: composition, GSM, width, unit, loss, supplier prices. Dictionaries: currency/rate, port, transport, packaging, HS/tax/refund, shipping company. | CRM owns customer facts; product-development owns supported style/material/size data; procurement reads suppliers and offerings. Do not assume all listed fields or dictionary/supplier write tools exist. Banking, credit and tax facts are not inferred from a supplier/customer name. |
| 2. 样品 / samples | Initial, confirmation, pre-production and shipping samples; request, sample BOM/cost, progress, customer comments, courier/tracking, sample inbound/outbound, color/handfeel/print/embroidery archives. | CRM sample requests/versions/feedback/shipments plus separately supported sales costing. A courier fact is not sample WMS posting, approval or a sample BOM. Do not claim full sample inventory or swatch archive support. |
| 3. 报价与成本 / costing and quotation | Fabric + trim + sewing + print/embroidery + washing + packing + freight + inspection + overhead + profit; FOB/EXW/CIF prices; bilingual quotation, style/size/MOQ/delivery/payment and history. | Use CRM Skill's costing/quotation Sales MCP flow. Preserve currency, rate, evidence and price versions. Sent quotation versions are immutable. Do not claim automatic full Incoterm conversion or bilingual export merely because a costing/quotation record exists. |
| 4. 订单 / sales orders | Buyer PO, style/color/size, quantity, price, amount, delivery, destination port, marks, special process; color/size/quantity/date changes; sampling→purchase→dyeing→trim→cutting→sewing→finishing→inspection→shipment progress. | Confirmed Sales MCP order and append-only change history; optional PI. Actual `styleVariantId` and quantities are required for downstream garment work. An order or its change history is not proof that production/warehouse/shipping progress is connected. |
| 5. BOM、工艺与技术资料 | Sewing/topstitch/wash/label position, per-garment consumption, cutting loss, total material, printing/embroidery/wash requirements, bilingual factory Tech Pack. | Product-development Skill owns published style/BOM/size-chart/Tech Pack and order-specific versions. MRP records frozen calculations, explicit variant applicability and units. Do not promise a document export without a producing tool/artifact. |
| 6. 采购 / purchasing | Fabric/trim PO, BOM-derived requirement, receipt, batch/shade difference, width/GSM IQC, material warehouse ledger, print/dye/embroidery processing. | ERP Skill's eleven Procurement MCP tools cover overview/supplier search, MRP, PO draft/preview/release, arrival, IQC, process draft/preview/release. Supplier/offering/inquiry creation is not exposed by these eleven tools. Receipt/IQC do not post WMS stock. |
| 7. 生产跟单 / production | Cutting notice, cutting plan/consumption, external garment production order, cutting/sewing/inbound counts, AQL and final inspection. | No current authoritative production MCP. Gather order, technical version, factory, operations, quantities, dates and evidence for a proposal. Do not send garment sewing through procurement process orders or invent progress reports. |
| 8. 仓库 WMS | Fabric/trim inbound/outbound and dye lot; garment inbound by order/color/size; picking/cartons/Packing List; stock, stagnant/remaining material; two-dimensional color-size inventory. | No current stock ledger/reservation/movement/pick/pack MCP. Gather warehouse/location, SKU/variant, lot, unit, quantity, movement time, source document and carton facts. Expected MRP/receipt quantity is not an available stock balance. |
| 9. 船务、报关、出货 | Shipment plan, booking instruction, PL, CI, customs facts, B/L, departure/arrival schedule, container type/count, CBM and gross/net weights. | No current confirmed shipment/customs MCP. Prepare drafts only from actual carton dimensions, counts, weights, buyer/order and booking evidence. Keep CI, PL, PI and B/L distinct. Do not infer loading, clearance or departure from a drafted document. |
| 10. 质检与测试 | Fabric/garment colorfastness, strength, flame tests; report archive; pre-production, in-process, final and SGS/ITS/customer third-party inspections. | Procurement IQC is supported. Lab applications, report center, AQL and full garment inspection disposition are not. Require test method, sample/batch, actual report/result, inspector and occurrence time; unknown is not passed. |
| 11. 财务 / finance | Order receivables, remittance, matching/overdue; factory/forwarder payables; L/C clauses/risk/presentation; FX settlement/gain/loss; refund document collection; freight/courier/testing/port/sample expenses. | No current financial posting/reconciliation MCP. Organize evidenced commercial/receipt/payment documents. Do not create ledger entries, mark paid, decide L/C compliance, invent exchange rates or tax refunds, or call a quotation margin realized profit. |
| 12. 单证与文档中心 | PO, quotation, PI, CI, PL, B/L, L/C, Tech Pack, swatches, inspection reports, customer email attachments. | Existing records have evidence references and selected frozen snapshots; no complete unified document-center MCP. Associate exact file, source, version, owner and business target; a link/reference is not a stored file or a verified provider message. Email uses separately authorized mailbox tools and CRM archival. |
| 13. 统计报表 / management reporting | Order/shipments, customer sales ranking, purchase cost, style profit, on-time/overdue and inventory capital. | Report only facts actually returned, stating period, scope, units, currency and missing denominators. No full current ERP reporting MCP. Quoted projected margin is not actual profit; empty reads are not proof of zero external sales, stock or debt. |

The PDF distinguishes a small export ERP (customers, samples, orders, BOM, purchasing, warehouse, documents and finance) from an integrated manufacturing ERP with cutting, process outsourcing, production progress, labor costing and workshop scan reporting. Do not silently expand a small trading business into factory scheduling. Ask for the operating model only when it affects the requested workflow.

For a missing module, return the known source facts, unresolved business decisions, needed authoritative objects/tools and the exact operation that remains unavailable. A Skill can support interpretation and proposal preparation before those tools exist; it cannot make the unsupported transaction real.
